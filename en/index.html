Questions?

Is it free?

You can practice for free with ads. An optional one-time in-app purchase removes ads. There is no subscription.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. RecallBloom is available on the App Store and Google Play. Download it from the store for your device.

Can I play if I'm new to Dual N-Back?

Three practice questions introduce matching positions and sounds. Use automatic difficulty adjustment or choose a level manually.

Where are my records saved?

Training records are stored on your device. No account registration is required.
